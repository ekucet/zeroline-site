The Zero Line — Support

A pixel-art murder mystery for iPhone and iPad. Five cases, one line around the world.

Contact / İletişim / Contacto

Report a problem or ask a question — open a GitHub issue (English, Türkçe or Español). Please include your device model and iOS version.

FAQ

I'm stuck on a case. Open the Notes (open questions) and the Deduction Board; tap the hint button in a scene for a nudge. Every case can be solved by observation and deduction.

Does the game need internet? No. It is fully offline, with no ads and no tracking.

How do I change the language? The game follows your device language (English, Türkçe, Español) and can be switched from the menu.

My progress disappeared. Progress is stored only on your device; deleting the app deletes it.
